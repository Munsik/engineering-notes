Engineering Notes collects the decision criteria I have built up designing and running Search, Security and Observability in enterprise environments.

## Author

Munsik Kim, Principal Solutions Architect at Elastic.
I spent more than ten years on the customer side, running systems in a security organization. Since then I have built and supported Search, Security and Observability projects hands-on as a consultant and as a solutions architect.

The content of this site is based on my own experience and opinions.

- GitHub: [Munsik](https://github.com/Munsik)

## Language

The articles are written in Korean first. English pages are translations; each one links to its Korean original, and the Korean page is authoritative if the two differ. A notice appears on an English page when the Korean original has changed since it was translated.
