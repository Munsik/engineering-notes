## Collections

An area is a technology. A collection takes one business problem and works through it in a series of articles, in order. The tag on each card is the collection's subject. These pages are English translations of the Korean originals; collections that are not translated yet are listed on the [Korean home page](/engineering-notes/).

## What these articles contain

Each article starts from a real business problem and, depending on the topic, covers three things:

- **Design decisions and reasons**: why this setup and these settings, and how they compare with the alternatives
- **How to apply it**: settings, APIs, queries and lab code you can follow as written
- **How to run it**: what can go wrong in production, and how to verify and respond
